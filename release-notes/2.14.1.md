- Downloads of emulators, cores, LaunchBox's database and arcade DATs pick up where they stopped after a dropped connection, and MAME's game list is checked against GitHub's checksum
- An emulator, core or media pack whose archive would write outside its folder, or fails halfway, leaves nothing behind
- Imports, checks and installs can't hang on a tool that prints a lot or waits for an answer, and closing a game that can't run also closes what it started
- Covers, logos and fan art are kept as small HEIC pictures and show without slowing the library down
- Manuals with a searchable text layer open without being redrawn
- Tidy Up and unzipped-game sizes count a hard-linked file once
