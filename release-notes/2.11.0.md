- Theme Editor: Settings › Cabinet Mode › Theme › Edit… changes every setting of a Cabinet theme beside a live preview with your own games, and saves it as a theme
- The preview shows the games, a game's page, the systems, the menu and the marquee screen, so each change shows at once where it applies
- Marquee Display: a cabinet with a second screen shows the selected game's marquee there, else its logo, and the system's logo on the systems
- The marquee screen comes and goes with its display, and Cabinet Mode carries on either way; a theme sets whether the marquee fits or fills it, and its background
- Game Art can show each game's cartridge or disc, or its box with the cartridge or disc at the corner, in every layout
